Welcome to Safari with Geofrey

My name is Geofrey, a local safari guide based in Tanzania. I am passionate about wildlife, nature and sharing the incredible beauty of Tanzania with visitors from around the world.

Through Safari with Geofrey, I help travellers discover Tanzania's extraordinary wildlife, beautiful landscapes and unforgettable safari experiences.
