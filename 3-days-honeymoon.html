Your Love Story, Written in the Wild

Imagine waking up to the sounds of the African wilderness, watching the golden sunrise stretch across endless savannah plains and sharing unforgettable moments surrounded by some of the world's most remarkable wildlife.

This carefully designed three-day Serengeti honeymoon experience combines romance, adventure and extraordinary natural beauty. From exciting game drives to the peaceful magic of a hot-air balloon flight, every moment offers an opportunity to create memories together.

Whether you are celebrating your honeymoon, an anniversary or simply the joy of travelling together, Serengeti offers an exceptional setting for a truly special escape.

Travel with local knowledge. With Safari with Geofrey, your journey is planned with personal attention, local destination knowledge and a passion for sharing Tanzania's remarkable wilderness.
Flexible safari departures

Choose Where Your Safari Begins

Your Serengeti honeymoon can be arranged from Zanzibar or Arusha, depending on your travel plans and preferred way of reaching the national park.

Starting from Zanzibar

Enjoy a convenient flight connection from the beautiful island of Zanzibar to the Serengeti. Begin your honeymoon adventure surrounded by spectacular landscapes and extraordinary African wildlife.

The Zanzibar departure option includes return flights between Zanzibar and Serengeti as specified in the USD 1,850 package.

Starting from Arusha

Begin your journey from Arusha, Tanzania's safari gateway. Travel towards the Serengeti by a suitable safari vehicle or arrange a flight connection, depending on your preferences and schedule.

The Arusha departure option is available on request. A personalised quotation will be provided based on your preferred transport arrangements.

Your three-day adventure

Safari Itinerary

Three unforgettable days of wildlife encounters, beautiful landscapes and special moments in the heart of the Serengeti.

Day 01

Arrival in Serengeti & Afternoon Game Drive

Arrive in the Serengeti from Zanzibar or Arusha, where your unforgettable honeymoon adventure begins. Meet your safari team and start discovering the remarkable beauty of Tanzania's most famous wildlife destination.

Enjoy an exciting game drive across the open plains, searching for lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest and other fascinating wildlife.

As the African sun begins to set, enjoy the beautiful Serengeti scenery before heading to your accommodation for dinner and a relaxing evening.

Overnight: Selected Serengeti accommodation.
Day 02

Hot-Air Balloon Safari & Full-Day Wildlife Adventure

Wake up early for one of the most extraordinary experiences of your honeymoon: a hot-air balloon safari over the magnificent Serengeti.

Watch the wilderness come alive as the first light of dawn illuminates the endless plains below. From above, appreciate the spectacular landscapes and wildlife of the Serengeti from a completely different perspective.

After your balloon experience, enjoy the special celebration and breakfast arranged as part of the balloon safari experience.

Continue exploring the Serengeti with an exciting game drive, discovering more wildlife and enjoying quality time together in the African wilderness.

Overnight: Selected Serengeti accommodation.
Day 03

Morning Safari & Departure

Enjoy your final morning in the Serengeti with an opportunity for another wildlife viewing experience, depending on your departure schedule.

Take in the beautiful scenery, capture your last photographs and appreciate the unforgettable moments you have shared during your honeymoon.

After the safari experience, transfer for your return journey to Zanzibar or Arusha according to your confirmed travel arrangements.

End of your Serengeti honeymoon safari.
