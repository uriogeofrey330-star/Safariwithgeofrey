Two Ways to Discover Panorama

1. Half-Day Panorama Experience

Perfect for travellers with limited time in Arusha. Enjoy an afternoon escape, scenic photography and beautiful sunset views before returning to your hotel.

  • 2:00 PM Pickup from your hotel in Arusha.
  • 3:00 PM Arrival at The Panorama.
  • 3:15 PM Explore the scenic surroundings and viewpoints.
  • 4:00 PM Photography and relaxation time.
  • 5:00 PM Enjoy the sunset atmosphere.
  • 6:30 PM Begin the return journey.
  • 7:00 PM Drop-off at your hotel.
Ideal for: Couples, friends, solo travellers and anyone looking for a memorable afternoon in Arusha.

2. Overnight Panorama Experience

Make more time for the scenery with an overnight escape. Enjoy the afternoon atmosphere, sunset, a peaceful evening and the beauty of the landscape the following morning.

DAY 1 – ARRIVAL AND SUNSET

  • 2:00 PM Pickup from your hotel in Arusha.
  • 3:00 PM Arrival and camping or accommodation arrangements.
  • 3:30 PM Explore the scenic viewpoints.
  • 4:30 PM Photography and relaxation.
  • 5:30 PM Enjoy the beautiful sunset.
  • 7:00 PM Dinner arrangements and evening relaxation.
  • 8:00 PM Enjoy a peaceful night outdoors.

DAY 2 – MORNING EXPERIENCE

  • 6:30 AM Enjoy the fresh morning atmosphere.
  • 7:00 AM Sunrise views and photography.
  • 8:00 AM Breakfast arrangements.
  • 9:00 AM Enjoy your final moments at the location.
  • 10:00 AM Begin the return journey to Arusha.
Ideal for: Couples, photography lovers, friends and travellers who prefer a relaxed outdoor escape. Overnight arrangements, meals and accommodation should be confirmed when planning your visit.
