4 Days Serengeti Balloon Safari

Discover the endless plains of the Serengeti, experience breathtaking wildlife encounters and enjoy the magic of a hot-air balloon safari above one of Africa's most extraordinary landscapes.

A Remarkable Serengeti Journey

Your 4-Day Safari Itinerary

Day 1 – Discover Central Serengeti (Seronera)

Begin your Serengeti adventure in the famous Seronera region, known for its beautiful open plains, river valleys and rich wildlife. Enjoy a game drive as you explore the area in search of lions, elephants, giraffes, zebras and other fascinating animals. Take in the remarkable scenery and the atmosphere of the African wilderness.

Day 2 – Journey Through the Serengeti

Continue your safari towards Northern Serengeti, enjoying wildlife viewing along the way. The journey offers changing landscapes and opportunities to encounter different animals. Arrive in the Kogatende area, a remarkable part of the Serengeti ecosystem known for its seasonal wildebeest migration.

Day 3 – Hot-Air Balloon Safari in Kogatende

Enjoy an unforgettable hot-air balloon experience over the Serengeti. Watch the landscape come alive from above as the morning light spreads across the plains. After landing, enjoy the balloon safari breakfast experience before continuing to explore the surrounding wilderness with your guide.

Day 4 – Final Game Drive and Departure

Enjoy your final moments in the Serengeti with a morning game drive, taking in the beautiful scenery and looking for more wildlife encounters. After your safari experience, continue with your planned departure arrangements.
