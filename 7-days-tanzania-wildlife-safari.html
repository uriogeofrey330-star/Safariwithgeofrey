Three Remarkable Destinations

Where Your Safari Takes You

Discover three extraordinary destinations in Tanzania's famous northern safari circuit.

01. Tarangire National Park

Famous for its impressive elephant herds, ancient baobab trees and beautiful landscapes. Tarangire offers excellent opportunities to observe wildlife around its seasonal rivers and open savannah.

02. Ngorongoro Crater

Explore one of Africa's most extraordinary natural landscapes. The crater floor supports a remarkable concentration of wildlife, including elephants, buffaloes, zebras, lions and the endangered black rhino.

03. Serengeti National Park

Discover the legendary Serengeti, known for its endless plains, extraordinary predator sightings and the spectacular Great Migration. Every game drive brings new possibilities.

Your Safari Experience

7-Day Safari Itinerary

An outline of your journey through Tanzania's most iconic wildlife destinations.

DAY 01

Arrival and Tarangire National Park

Begin your Tanzania adventure with a journey to Tarangire National Park. Enjoy a game drive through landscapes dotted with ancient baobab trees and look out for elephants, giraffes, zebras, wildebeest and a variety of bird species.

DAY 02

Explore the Beauty of Tarangire

Enjoy another opportunity to explore Tarangire's remarkable wildlife habitats. Discover the park's diverse landscapes and observe animals gathering around its seasonal water sources. Enjoy a memorable day in the African wilderness.

DAY 03

Journey to the Ngorongoro Highlands

Travel towards the Ngorongoro Conservation Area, enjoying the changing scenery along the way. Discover the beautiful highland landscapes and prepare for your exploration of the world-famous crater.

DAY 04

Ngorongoro Crater Safari

Descend into the spectacular Ngorongoro Crater for an unforgettable wildlife experience. Search for lions, elephants, buffaloes, zebras, hippos and, with luck, the endangered black rhino.

DAY 05

Journey to Serengeti National Park

Continue your adventure towards the legendary Serengeti. Enjoy wildlife viewing along the journey as the landscapes open into vast plains. Arrive in the Serengeti and experience the remarkable atmosphere of the African savannah.

DAY 06

Full-Day Serengeti Wildlife Exploration

Spend the day exploring the Serengeti with your safari guide. Look out for lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants, giraffes and other wildlife. Depending on the season, you may also have opportunities to witness parts of the Great Migration.

DAY 07

Morning Game Drive and Departure

Enjoy your final safari moments with a morning game drive, subject to your departure arrangements. After your wildlife experience, begin your onward journey according to your confirmed travel plan.
