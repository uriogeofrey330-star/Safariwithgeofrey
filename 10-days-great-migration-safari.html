Experience the Great Migration in Northern Serengeti

Ten days dedicated to exploring two remarkable regions of the Serengeti ecosystem.

The Great Migration is one of the most remarkable wildlife spectacles on Earth. Every year, vast herds of wildebeest and zebras move across the Serengeti ecosystem in search of fresh grazing and water.


This ten-day safari focuses on the spectacular Northern Serengeti, with time to explore Kogatende and Lamai. Enjoy extended game drives, discover different wildlife habitats and experience the beautiful landscapes surrounding the Mara River.


With Safari with Geofrey, you can explore these remarkable wilderness areas with a local guide who is passionate about Tanzania's wildlife and natural heritage.

Two Remarkable Safari Regions

Explore the diverse landscapes and wildlife experiences of Kogatende and Lamai.

01. Kogatende

Located in Northern Serengeti, Kogatende is known for its beautiful open plains, rocky landscapes and the seasonal movement of wildebeest and zebras. The Mara River area offers opportunities to search for wildlife and, when conditions allow, observe river crossings.

02. Lamai

Lamai offers a remarkable Northern Serengeti wilderness experience, with scenic landscapes, rocky outcrops and open grasslands. Explore the area on game drives and look for resident wildlife and seasonal migration herds.

Understanding the Great Migration: The location and movement of the herds change throughout the year. River crossings are natural events and cannot be guaranteed on a particular day or at a particular time. Your safari route and game drives will be planned around seasonal wildlife movements and current conditions.
Your Ten-Day Adventure

10-Day Great Migration Itinerary

Seven days exploring Kogatende followed by three days discovering Lamai.

DAY 01

Arrival in Northern Serengeti – Kogatende

Arrive in the Kogatende region and begin your Northern Serengeti adventure. Enjoy your first game drive through the surrounding landscapes, looking for wildebeest, zebras, elephants, giraffes and predators.

DAY 02

Exploring Kogatende and the Mara River Area

Spend the day exploring Kogatende and its surrounding wildlife habitats. Visit suitable game-viewing areas around the Mara River and look for seasonal migration herds and resident wildlife.

DAY 03

Following the Great Migration

Continue your search for the migrating wildebeest and zebras. Observe the behaviour of the herds and the predators that follow them across the Northern Serengeti. Every game drive offers a different wildlife experience.

DAY 04

Full-Day Wildlife Photography in Kogatende

Enjoy an extended day of wildlife viewing and photography. Look for lions, leopards, cheetahs, elephants and other species while exploring the region's beautiful landscapes.

DAY 05

Another Day in the Northern Serengeti

Explore different areas of Kogatende based on wildlife movements and local conditions. Your guide will help identify promising locations for observing the migration and other wildlife encounters.

DAY 06

Great Migration and Mara River Exploration

Continue exploring the Mara River ecosystem and nearby plains. Depending on the season, water levels and the location of the herds, you may have opportunities to observe wildebeest gathering near river crossing points.

DAY 07

Final Full Day Exploring Kogatende

Enjoy your final day in Kogatende with more opportunities for wildlife viewing and photography. Discover the region's changing scenery before continuing towards Lamai.

DAY 08

Journey from Kogatende to Lamai

Continue your Northern Serengeti adventure towards Lamai. Enjoy wildlife viewing along the route and discover the changing landscapes of this remarkable part of the Serengeti ecosystem.

DAY 09

Discover the Wilderness of Lamai

Spend the day exploring Lamai and its surrounding wildlife habitats. Look for predators, elephants, giraffes, zebras and seasonal migration herds. Enjoy the beauty and tranquillity of Northern Serengeti.

DAY 10

Final Game Drive and Departure

Enjoy your final safari experience in Lamai, subject to your departure arrangements. After your last wildlife adventure, continue with your confirmed onward travel arrangements.
